Risk Management

Position sizing controls how much you risk per trade without touching entries or stops — it does not change the strategy's win rate, only the dollar amount at stake. Every trade plan also builds in a partial-profit-at-TP1 + move-stop-to-breakeven step to cut risk on the runner leg.
